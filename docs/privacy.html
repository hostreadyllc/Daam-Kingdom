Daam Kingdom is operated by HostReady LLC. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Daam Kingdom Android app (com.hostready.daamkingdom) and the Daam Kingdom website.

Information we process

  • Username: the display name you enter for a match.
  • Game information: room code, board position, messages chosen from the preset list, match status and a reconnection token.
  • Technical information: our hosting and security providers may process IP address, device type, browser information and request logs to operate and protect the service.

We do not create user accounts and do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment information.

App permissions

  • Internet: needed to load the game and play matches online.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play. You can turn it off with the Vibration button in the game.

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location or your files.

How information is used

Information is used only to create multiplayer rooms, keep matches in sync, reconnect players, run the Daam Bot, prevent abuse and keep the service reliable. Daam Kingdom does not sell personal information, does not show third-party advertising and does not use game information for behavioural advertising. The Android app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

Storage and retention

Inactive room records are deleted automatically after about 24 hours. Security and infrastructure logs may be kept for a limited period by our hosting providers. If you prefer to stay anonymous, do not use your legal name as your username.

Sharing

Information is processed by the service providers needed to host and secure the game. It is not shared with data brokers or advertisers. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Security

All connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS).

Children

The game does not ask for a date of birth, contact list, precise location, photos or payment information. Parents or guardians should supervise children and help them choose a username that does not identify them.

Your choices

You can stop using the game or uninstall the app at any time. Because no account is created, room information cannot normally be linked back to you after the room expires. For a privacy question or a deletion request about an active room, email us with the room code.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date on this page.
